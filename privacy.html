Privacy Policy

Last updated: Expo 2026 exhibition cycle

1. Who we are

Expo 2026 is the annual school exhibition platform where students submit, review and showcase projects. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data we collect

  • Student accounts: name, email, grade/class, admission/roll numbers, partner names and roll numbers, optional profile picture (Google sign-in), and hashed passwords (we never store plain-text passwords).
  • Project submissions: title, description, category, tags, images/URLs, demo and repository links, submission timestamps, review status and reviewer notes.
  • Visitor/voting data: an anonymous browser visitor ID used to enforce one vote per device on the "Most loved" leaderboard. No personal identity is collected from visitors.
  • Admin accounts: administrator usernames, hashed passwords, roles, and creation/update timestamps. Multiple admin users may be created, edited or removed by an authorized administrator.
  • Activity/audit log: actions such as approvals, rejections, edits, deletions, and admin sign-ins, with timestamps.

3. Exhibition schedule and dates

Exhibition dates, milestones and timings (submission deadline, review window, exhibition day, awards ceremony) are configured by administrators and shown on the public schedule page. These may change; the published schedule is the authoritative source.

4. How we use data

We use collected data to run the exhibition: accept and review submissions, display approved projects publicly, manage featured projects, operate voting, administer accounts, and maintain an audit trail. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Google sign-in is used only for authentication.

5. Admin user management

Authorized administrators can create, edit (username/password/role) and delete administrator accounts, and view all registered student accounts. The last remaining admin account cannot be deleted, and an admin cannot delete their own account. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

6. Student account management

Administrators can view all student accounts and remove an account when necessary. Students may update their own profile details. Voting is tied to a student account (one vote per account) and can be changed or withdrawn.

7. Data retention

Project submissions and accounts are retained for the duration of the exhibition cycle and may be archived or cleared by an administrator using the control center. Audit logs may be cleared by administrators.

8. Security

Authentication uses signed, expiring tokens (admin sessions 7 days; student sessions 14 days). Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2. Admin routes require a valid admin token. Preview/sample exhibits are display-only and never written to real project data.

9. Your rights

Students may request correction or deletion of their account and submissions through their teacher or administrator. Visitors may clear their browser data to reset their anonymous voting ID.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact the exhibition organizers / school administration.
